The Problem

Computer vision projects stall before they ever reach production

Stitched together across vendors

Detecting defects, tracking people and objects, reading gauges — doing this today means stitching together separate tools for data labeling, model training, deployment, and monitoring, usually across multiple vendors or cloud services.
